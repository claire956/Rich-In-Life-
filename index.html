The Show

What does it actually mean to be rich, once money is off the table?

Hosted by Chris Ehrenfeld, Rich in Life sits down with entrepreneurs, athletes, and people who've built something extraordinary — not just to talk about how they got there, but to ask a harder question.

Every guest gets a real conversation, not a highlight reel. We go past the achievement story into the character behind it. What made you who you are. What you'd tell yourself at 80, looking back, if the version of "proud" had nothing to do with your bank account.

Why Guests Say Yes
01 — DEPTH

Real depth, not a drive-by

Guests get a full, unhurried conversation broken across a multi-part arc, so your story actually gets room to breathe instead of getting cut for time.

02 — TRUST

No gotcha questions

This isn't a hot-take show. It's built around story first, insight second.

03 — EXPOSURE

A different kind of exposure

Business podcasts ask about strategy. Sports shows ask about the game. We ask what it cost you, what you learned, and what you'd want your kids to know. That's the conversation people remember and share.

04 — CRAFT

Recorded in the Bold Studio

A state-of-the-art podcast studio in Chapel Hill, built for a clean, professional final product.

05 — PEER

A peer, not a personality

Chris Ehrenfeld built, invested in, and led businesses himself, so the conversation comes from experience, not a script.

06 — WHO'S FEATURED

Founders, CEOs, athletes

People redefining success on their own terms, alongside experts in health, wellness, and finance who bring practical tools for actually living the life the stories point to.

No one reaches the end of their life wishing they'd built a bigger business. They wish they'd built a richer one. Rich in Life is the conversation about how.
